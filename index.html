Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UDISE code and establishment year of S.S. +2 High School, Gola? ▼

State Subsidized (S.S.) +2 High School, Gola (District Ramgarh, Jharkhand) was established in 1940. Its official UDISE Code is 20240701206, and it is affiliated with the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi.

Which classes and +2 streams are offered at the school? ▼

The school operates an integrated 4-wing academic structure from Class 1 to Class 12, including Primary (1–5), Upper Primary (6–8), Matric Secondary (9–10), and +2 Intermediate (Science PCM/PCB, Arts & Commerce).

Where is S.S. +2 High School, Gola located? ▼

The campus is situated near the Community Health Centre, Heramdaga Road, Bantara, Gola Block, District Ramgarh, Jharkhand — 829110.

What scholarships and lab facilities are available for students? ▼

Students benefit from e-Kalyan Jharkhand, Savitribai Phule Kishori Samriddhi Yojana (₹40,000), CMMSS, Guruji Student Credit Card, and Class 8 free cycles, alongside separate Physics/Chemistry/Biology labs, ICT/IT computer labs, Smart Classrooms, and NCC.
